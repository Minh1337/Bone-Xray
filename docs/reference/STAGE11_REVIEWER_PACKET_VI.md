## Blind review safeguards

1. Reviewer queries and labels must be newly authored or selected independently; do not reuse the agent-authored dev set as blind data.
   Before lock, a separate data custodian checks exact/normalized duplicates, shared case/query-family or evidence assertions, and reviewed near-duplicates against every viewed dev/regression and prior blind set. Preserve the method, overlap count, disposition and hash; keep overlap details out of the tuning team's view until labels and configuration are frozen.
2. Do not inspect existing retrieval reports until query set, expected status, acceptable evidence groups, exact spans, and rubric are signed and hashed. The existing dev and V2 results have been viewed and V2 is regression-only.
3. Keep the retrieval config, implementation, corpus, index manifest, and rubric unchanged between lock and run. Any change after opening results requires a new blind set.
4. Set pass/stop thresholds and risk tolerance before retrieval results are opened. Report counts and denominators by language, topic, source, and query intent.
5. Do not enter patient identifiers, real case narratives, or images into this packet.

Use JSON arrays in the evidence/quote/qualifier fields. For more than one valid supporting passage, record alternative acceptable evidence groups so evaluator precision does not treat valid alternatives as errors.

The reviewer protocol defines disjoint metric populations: fully answerable, partially answerable, in-scope unanswerable, and out-of-scope. Partial answers are tracked separately and should route to insufficient evidence under the current conservative rubric; out-of-scope items are excluded from the in-scope unanswerable denominator. Report raw counts and pre-specified uncertainty intervals at the independent sampling-unit level; paraphrases of one underlying fact must not be treated as independent observations. See [population definitions, inter-rater agreement and uncertainty methods](STAGE11_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_PROTOCOL_VI.md).

## Scope and safety limits

- This RAG stage retrieves candidate passages; it has no answer-generation API or chatbot. `answer_ready` is always `false`.
- Audit PASS checks artifact integrity and provenance only. It does not establish clinical correctness, license interpretation beyond the recorded checks, citation entailment, or safety.
- The experimental strict lexical evidence filter has high positive abstention and does not establish entailment. Keep it disabled for end users.
- Sources have narrow scopes. In particular, Bone-RADS concerns incidental solitary lesions on CT/MRI in adults, and the British Sarcoma Group guideline reflects UK pathways; reviewers should assess fit to the intended Vietnamese setting.
- BTXRD includes classes named `osteofibroma` and `synovial osteochondroma`, but the published names alone do not provide an operational case definition or prove uniform histopathology. WHO ICD-O-3.1 lists `osteofibroma` under M-9262/0 `ossifying fibroma`, a nomenclature/coding alias; a 2024 CC BY 4.0 human OFD review also recounts historical use of “ossifying fibroma” before “osteofibrous dysplasia” was adopted. Together these show terminology depends on classification and historical context, not how BTXRD applied the label to individual images. Preserve the source label and alias provenance without silently relabeling cases. A SAGE case report uses “synovial osteochondroma” in a narrower solitary, synovial-lined context under CC BY-NC 3.0; it was not ingested and does not establish the dataset mapping. See the [dated online evidence review](stage11_online_evidence_review_20261028.md).
- New nomenclature screen (2026-10-29): a CC BY 4.0 single case of mandibular ossifying fibroma was excluded from ingestion because its jaw-specific case and differential do not define the BTXRD `osteofibroma` label or long-bone OFD. The current IARC/WHO fifth-edition structure and a peer-reviewed mouse-tumour review are recorded as context; neither licenses an automatic class mapping. Keep `unverified_dataset_label_mapping` until independent specialist adjudication. Full rationale and sources: [online evidence review](stage11_online_evidence_review_20261028.md).
- Before real or identifiable patient data are introduced, require an approved purpose/authority, data-controller responsibilities, minimization, access controls, retention/deletion plan, incident process, and any required consent/notice or sharing/transfer review. Do not enter patient identifiers, real case narratives, or images into the reviewer packet or blind-label CSV.
- **Local code-boundary review (2026-10-29; static inspection only):** the launcher binds Uvicorn to `127.0.0.1`, accepts one worker, caps uploads at 30 MiB/25 million pixels, decodes supported image formats, and holds uploaded pixels in a temporary directory during prediction. Pending predictions (metadata, image hash/name, scores) are kept in process memory for up to ten minutes; the database stores the hash/metadata/scores and comments/corrections, but not the uploaded pixels. Case, comment, correction, list and dashboard endpoints have no authentication or per-user authorization. SQLite is local and unencrypted, has no configured retention schedule, and WAL/backup deletion behavior has not been reviewed. The loopback binding limits network reachability for the documented launcher; it does not provide user authentication, protect data from other local processes/accounts, or make real patient data appropriate. Keep the demo local-only and use synthetic/non-identifiable inputs until an approved security/privacy review defines authentication, access, encryption, retention, incident response and verified deletion requirements. ## Ghi chú cho cổng entailment: ba câu hỏi cần chấm riêng

Khi duyệt retrieval, hãy tách ba kết luận: passage có liên quan đúng câu hỏi; nội dung passage có trực tiếp hỗ trợ claim và qualifier cần thiết; claim có đúng theo reference standard/evidence hay không. Nghiên cứu radiology QA 2025 chấm riêng ba yếu tố này và vẫn quan sát câu trả lời sai dù đã truy xuất ngữ cảnh phù hợp; do đó relevance hoặc citation presence không đủ làm bằng chứng entailment. Đây là nguyên tắc thiết kế rubric, không phải ngưỡng định lượng chuyển giao cho BoneScope. [Nghiên cứu RaR](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-02250-5). Cổng answer-generation vẫn chưa áp dụng vì hệ thống hiện chưa có answer-generation API.

Tổng quan scoping 2026 của JMIR mapped 157 healthcare RAG/GraphRAG studies, nhưng eligibility yêu cầu system sinh output; vì vậy không xem là benchmark retrieval-only cho BoneScope. Tỷ lệ đánh giá độc lập retrieval 29,9%, fine-grained evidence verification 14,0%, citation/source correctness 7,6%, formal safety 28,7%; các mẫu số/construct riêng và đây chỉ là coverage mô tả. Dùng như lý do không gộp các lớp đánh giá. Trong gate 1/3, chấm: (a) retrieved passage relevance, (b) retrieval/evidence group coverage, (c) citation exists and points to correct source/location, (d) claim-level support including polarity/qualifier/context, (e) truth against external reference when applicable; mark generation-only layers `not applicable` until a generator exists.
